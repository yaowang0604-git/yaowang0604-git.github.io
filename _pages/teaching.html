--- layout: archive title: "Teaching and Mentoring" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- I have served as a Teaching Assistant at **The University of Texas at Austin** since August 2022. My duties include holding office hours, grading homework, and maintaining sample homework solutions. My teaching philosophy is to encourage students to think independently while using examples, related results, and broader mathematical connections to deepen their understanding. ## Graduate preliminary-exam courses - **Algebra I** — Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026 - **Differential Topology** — Spring 2025 ## Undergraduate courses - **Topology II** — Spring 2024 - **Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory** — Spring 2024 - **Algebraic Structure I** — Spring 2023, Fall 2023 - **Algebraic Structure II** — Spring 2023 - **Matrices/Matrix Calculations – Computer Science** — Fall 2022 ## Mentoring **Directed Reading Program, UT Austin — Fall 2025** I mentored **Xinbo Li** in a reading of Robin Hartshorne's *Algebraic Geometry*. The project concluded with the presentation > *From Classical to Modern: Bridging Varieties and Schemes.* The reading introduced the transition from classical algebraic varieties to the scheme-theoretic language used in modern algebraic geometry.
