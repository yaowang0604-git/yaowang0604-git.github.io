## Seminar Organization

### Junior Geometry and QFT Seminar

**Organizer, The University of Texas at Austin**  
Fall 2024; Spring 2025; Fall 2025

A joint seminar for mathematicians and physicists interested in geometry and theoretical physics.

Past topics include quantum integrable systems, topological quantum field theory, generalized symmetry, von Neumann algebras, the AGT correspondence, Bridgeland stability, Seiberg–Witten invariants, 2d and 3d mirror symmetry, Chern–Simons theory, chiral algebras, and the geometric Langlands program.
