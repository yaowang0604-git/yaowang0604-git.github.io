--- layout: archive title: "Publications" permalink: /publications/ author_profile: true --- ## Preprints and manuscripts **Pierrick Bousseau, Bernd Siebert, and Wang Yao** *Quantum Geometry of Mirror Landau–Ginzburg Model of* \((\mathbb P^2,E)\), preprint, to be posted on arXiv (2026). This work proves the quantum closed-string mirror-symmetry conjecture for \[ K_{\mathbb P^2} \] proposed by Aganagic–Cheng–Dijkgraaf–Krefl–Vafa. The two main ingredients are: 1. an intrinsic interpretation of quantum periods using deformation quantization; and 2. the Gross–Siebert construction of the mirror proper Landau–Ginzburg model. The approach extends directly to local toric del Pezzo surfaces. The same framework also reformulates the quantum open-string conjecture in terms of a punctured–open correspondence.
