Manuscripts and Preprints

Pierrick Bousseau, Bernd Siebert, and Wang Yao,
Quantum Geometry of Mirror Landau–Ginzburg Model of \((\mathbb{P}^2,E)\), manuscript in preparation (2026).

This work proves the quantum closed-string mirror-symmetry conjecture for local \(\mathbb{P}^2\) proposed by Aganagic–Cheng–Dijkgraaf–Krefl–Vafa, using an intrinsic interpretation of quantum periods via deformation quantization and the Gross–Siebert construction of the mirror proper Landau–Ginzburg model. The method extends directly to local toric del Pezzo surfaces.
