--- layout: archive title: "Publications" permalink: /publications/ author_profile: true --- ## Preprints and Publications **Pierrick Bousseau, Bernd Siebert, and Wang Yao**, *Quantum Geometry of Mirror Landau–Ginzburg Model of* \((\mathbb{P}^2,E)\), preprint, to be posted on arXiv (2026). - We give the first mathematical proof of the quantum closed-string mirror-symmetry conjecture for \(K_{\mathbb{P}^2}\) proposed by Aganagic–Cheng–Dijkgraaf–Krefl–Vafa. - The proof uses an intrinsic definition of quantum periods via deformation quantization together with the Gross–Siebert construction of the mirror proper Landau–Ginzburg model. - The method extends to local toric del Pezzo surfaces. - We reformulate the quantum open-string conjecture as a punctured–open correspondence in enumerative geometry.
